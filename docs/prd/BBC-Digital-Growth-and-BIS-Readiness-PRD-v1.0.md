## 1. Executive Summary

BBC Digital Growth & BIS Readiness PRD v1.0 defines Bintaro Business Centre’s digital system as an SEO-first acquisition engine today and a future business intelligence foundation for BBC operations.

The current priority is not to build a full business system immediately. The current priority is to strengthen BBC’s website as a revenue-producing SEO asset that can generate qualified leads for:

- Service office / sewa kantor
- Virtual office
- Pendirian PT and legal services
- Meeting room
- Trust and support paths that improve conversion confidence

The future direction is to connect BBC website data into a BBC unit-level BIS covering marketing, sales, finance, operations, and HRD. This future BIS should eventually connect upward into Tasneem Group Platform.

The website must not be migrated into Tasneem Platform at this phase. The website remains the acquisition surface. Tasneem Platform and future BBC BIS become the data, CRM, reporting, and operating intelligence layers.

**Strategic principle:**

```txt
BBC Website = acquisition surface
BBC CRM / WA Inbox = lead and sales workflow
BBC BIS = unit-level operating intelligence
Tasneem Group Platform = group-level intelligence and shared operating system
```

## 2. Business Context

Bintaro Business Centre is a business infrastructure provider located in Pesanggrahan, Jakarta Selatan. BBC provides physical and administrative business support services, including:

- Service office / furnished office rooms
- Virtual office / business address
- Meeting room
- Legal and company formation support
- Receptionist and mail handling
- Business address support in Jakarta Selatan jurisdiction

BBC’s business model is lead-driven and trust-driven. The user often searches with commercial intent but still needs clarity before contacting sales. Therefore, the website must educate, qualify, reassure, and move the user toward a measurable action.

BBC’s digital strategy must support both short-term revenue and long-term operational intelligence.

Short-term:

```txt
Search visibility → qualified traffic → WhatsApp/form/phone lead → sales follow-up → conversion
```

Long-term:

```txt
Traffic source → lead source → service intent → sales outcome → finance result → operational implication → management dashboard
```

## 8. SEO Architecture

### 8.1 Core SEO Principle

BBC uses a structured SEO system.

The governing principle is:

```txt
1 URL = 1 Angle
```

Each important URL must have:

- Page Type
- Family
- Primary Entity
- Angle
- Intent
- Internal linking role
- Conversion role
- Trust proof requirement

No page should be created simply because a query appears in GSC. A new page requires distinct intent, distinct angle, and clear internal linking role.

## 11. Content Production Strategy

### 11.1 Current Problem

The BBC project has spent significant time on SEO tooling, trust injection, GSC cleanup, and Quality Gate development. These are useful, but content production has slowed down.

The next stage must rebalance the project toward actual SEO content production.

### 11.2 Content Production Goal

The goal is to produce and improve pages that can rank, assist conversion, and strengthen entity authority.

Priority order:

1. Improve existing high-value money pages.
2. Upgrade important weapon pages with better structure and mid-page CTA.
3. Produce missing high-intent SEO pages if structurally justified.
4. Strengthen support pages only when trust gaps hurt conversion or Quality Gate.
5. Avoid over-optimizing internal tools before new content is produced.

### 11.3 Content Production Rules

Every new content task must define:

- URL
- Page Type
- Entity
- Angle
- Intent
- Destination money page
- Required trust proof
- Internal links in/out
- CTA role
- Measurement role

### 11.4 Writing Requirements

Content must follow:

- Answer-first opening
- Semantic triple clarity
- Entity placement discipline
- Short paragraphs for mobile
- Structured blocks instead of wall-of-text
- FAQ where useful
- Comparison table where useful
- CTA in appropriate locations
- No fake urgency
- No unsupported legal claim
- No AI fluff

## 12. Entity SEO Requirements

### 12.1 Entity Placement

Each important page must define:

- Primary entity
- Supporting entities
- Location entity
- Service entity
- Action entity
- Trust entity
- Destination money page

Primary entity should appear naturally in:

- URL
- Title
- H1
- Opening paragraph
- First visible content block
- At least one H2/H3 when relevant
- Internal anchor text or surrounding sentence
- CTA label when relevant
- Image alt when relevant
- Schema when relevant

### 12.2 Answer-First Structure

Money and weapon pages must answer user intent early.

Bad opening patterns:

- “Di era modern…”
- “Saat ini banyak bisnis…”
- “Solusi terbaik…”
- “Kami hadir…”
- “Dalam dunia bisnis yang terus berkembang…”

Good opening pattern:

```txt
Subject → Predicate → Object
```

Example:

```txt
Bintaro Business Centre menyediakan sewa kantor siap pakai di Pesanggrahan, Jakarta Selatan, dekat Pintu Tol Veteran untuk perusahaan yang membutuhkan ruang kerja operasional dengan alamat resmi DKI Jakarta.
```

### 12.3 Trust Visibility

BBC trust proof must be visible where it supports conversion or authority.

Allowed trust proof includes:

- PT. Ganesha Dwipaya Bhakti
- Bintaro Business Centre
- Operating since 2007
- Jl. RC. Veteran No. 1-i
- Pesanggrahan, Jakarta Selatan
- Pintu Tol Veteran
- RC Veteran Raya
- 1,600 m² building
- 4 floors
- 72 office units
- Service office
- Virtual office
- Meeting room
- Receptionist
- Mail handling
- Google Maps location
- Legal/KBLI clarification when relevant

Forbidden claims:

- Guaranteed approval
- 100% approved
- Pasti lolos
- Terbaik without proof
- Termurah without proof
- Fake urgency
- Fake review
- Unsupported legal conclusion

## 13. Internal Linking Strategy

### 13.1 Core Principle

Internal linking controls:

- Crawl path
- Authority flow
- Entity relationship
- Silo clarity
- Conversion routing
- Indexation support

BBC uses soft silo architecture. Entity-based silo is preferred over rigid folder-based silo.

### 13.2 Silo Targets

| Silo | Primary Money Target |
|---|---|
| Sewa Kantor | `/sewa-kantor` |
| Virtual Office | `/virtual-office` |
| Legal | `/legal/pendirian-pt-jakarta-selatan` |
| Trust / Support | Route back to relevant service page |

### 13.3 Internal Link Rules

Weapon pages:

- Link to relevant money page 3–4 times where natural.
- First money link should appear within first 300 words when natural.
- Include mid-content soft CTA.
- Include final helpful CTA.
- Link to hub/related pages only when useful.

Support pages:

- Must not become dead-end pages.
- Should route users to relevant service/contact/location path.
- Should include trust proof.

Money pages:

- Receive authority.
- Outbound links should be controlled.
- Can link to support/trust page only where it helps conversion.

### 13.4 Anchor Text Rules

Good anchors:

- “layanan sewa kantor BBC”
- “kantor siap pakai di Jakarta Selatan”
- “virtual office Jakarta Selatan”
- “alamat bisnis untuk PT dan CV”
- “pendirian PT dengan domisili Jakarta Selatan”
- “lokasi BBC dekat Pintu Tol Veteran”

Avoid:

- “klik di sini”
- “baca selengkapnya” without context
- repeated exact match anchors
- keyword stuffing
- unrelated cross-silo links

## 14. Quality Gate System

### 14.1 Purpose

Quality Gate is a diagnostic tool, not the business objective.

It should help the team decide:

- Which page has weak trust proof
- Which page has weak entity placement
- Which page lacks answer-first structure
- Which page lacks interaction path
- Which page risks passive behavior
- Which page needs CTA or internal link injection

### 14.2 Scoring Dimensions

Current dimensions:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Entity Placement | URL/title/H1/opening/headings/anchors/CTA/schema alignment |
| Answer First | Whether intent is answered early and clearly |
| Interaction | CTA, WhatsApp, form, phone, maps, FAQ, internal money link |
| Task Success | Whether user can complete the intended task |
| Trust Visibility | Visible factual proof points |
| SERP Optimization | Title/meta clarity, uniqueness, CTR potential |

### 14.3 Badge Logic

Current alert badges include:

- `trust_weak`
- `overall_fair`
- `answer_fair`
- `entity_fair`

Badges should guide editorial decisions. They should not force unnatural writing.

### 14.4 Quality Gate Governance

Do not optimize pages merely to pass a threshold.

If a support page has a small residual `entity_fair` because its H1 is trust-oriented, do not force it into a service page unless the page angle supports that change.
